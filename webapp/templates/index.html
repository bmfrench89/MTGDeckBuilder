A theory deck is a list you want to try on paper: the best build from everything you own, a deck you're thinking of breaking down, or a commander you haven't sleeved. It claims no copies, so it never makes your real decks' cards contested.

To add one, use Move to the Lab in a deck's menu on Your decks, or ask a session for a theory build.
