# Tinybones analogs — verified verbatim from Scryfall, 2026-08-30
#
# THE QUESTION. The player asked for "a Commander with mechanics similar to
# Tinybones," for their brother. They own no Tinybones printing, so nothing in
# the collection anchored the comparison and memory was the only alternative —
# which is exactly the anti-pattern tool-contract.md §5.1 names.
#
# HOW THIS WAS MADE. 38 card names (30 via data/reference/verify-queue.txt on a
# push, 8 more via workflow_dispatch) went through `carddb.py --verify` on a
# GitHub runner: deck-verify.yml runs 33329008762, 33329055024, 33329105195,
# 33329112713 and the Tergrid re-dispatch. Field data for the three commanders
# below came from the same workflow's edhrec.py --snapshot step and is committed
# under data/reference/field/. Nothing here is from memory.
#
# MEMORY WAS WRONG, AND THIS IS WHY THE RUN HAPPENED. Before verification the
# model's recollection of Tinybones, Trinket Thief was "whenever an opponent
# discards a card, you may pay {1}: draw a card." The real card is a delayed
# once-per-turn end-step trigger that also costs 1 life. Same flavour, different
# card, and a deck built on the remembered version would over-value wheel
# effects that dump many cards at once.
#
# ==================================================================
# THE BASELINE — all four Tinybones cards, verbatim
# ==================================================================
#
# Tinybones, Trinket Thief  {1}{B}  — Legendary Creature — Skeleton Rogue
#   identity B · commander-legal · JMP 17
#   | At the beginning of each end step, if an opponent discarded a card this
#   | turn, you draw a card and you lose 1 life.
#   | {4}{B}{B}: Each opponent with no cards in hand loses 10 life.
#   TWO HALVES: (a) a payoff that fires ONCE per turn cycle no matter how many
#   cards were discarded, and (b) an emptied-hand alt-wincon. Any "similar"
#   commander is similar on one half or the other, rarely both.
#
# Tinybones, Bauble Burglar  {1}{B}  — Legendary Creature — Skeleton Rogue
#   identity B · commander-legal · FDN 72
#   | Whenever an opponent discards a card, exile it from their graveyard with a
#   | stash counter on it.
#   | During your turn, you may play cards you don't own with stash counters on
#   | them from exile, and mana of any type can be spent to cast those spells.
#   | {3}{B}, {T}: Each opponent discards a card. Activate only as a sorcery.
#   The most-played Tinybones by a wide margin (10,674 EDHREC decks vs 4,960 for
#   Trinket Thief). Unlike Trinket Thief it scales per discard AND carries its
#   own repeatable discard engine, so it needs less enchantment support.
#
# Tinybones, the Pickpocket  {B}  — Legendary Creature — Skeleton Rogue
#   identity B · commander-legal · OTJ 109
#   | Deathtouch
#   | Whenever Tinybones deals combat damage to a player, you may cast target
#   | nonland permanent card from that player's graveyard, and mana of any type
#   | can be spent to cast that spell.
#   The THEFT half only — no discard payoff. Wants evasion, not hand attack.
#
# Tinybones Joins Up  {B}  — Legendary Enchantment (not a commander)
#   identity B · commander-legal · OTJ 108
#   | When Tinybones Joins Up enters, any number of target players each discard
#   | a card.
#   | Whenever a legendary creature you control enters, any number of target
#   | players each mill a card and lose 1 life.
#
# ==================================================================
# THE ANALOGS — ranked by how much of Tinybones they reproduce
# ==================================================================
#
# 1. Tergrid, God of Fright // Tergrid's Lantern  {3}{B}{B} // {3}{B}
#    identity B · commander-legal · KHM 112 · Legendary Creature — God //
#    Legendary Artifact
#    | Menace
#    | Whenever an opponent sacrifices a nontoken permanent or discards a
#    | permanent card, you may put that card from a graveyard onto the
#    | battlefield under your control.
#    // {T}: Target player loses 3 life unless they sacrifice a nonland
#    | permanent of their choice or discard a card.
#    | {3}{B}: Untap Tergrid's Lantern.
#    SAME AXIS, BIGGER PAYOFF: punishes the opponent's hand, but converts the
#    discard into a permanent on YOUR board instead of a card draw. The back
#    face is a standalone repeatable discard engine, so the card is never dead.
#    57% inclusion in the Trinket Thief field — Tinybones players run Tergrid.
#    CAVEAT worth stating out loud for a gift deck: Tergrid has a real
#    reputation as a miserable card to sit across from.
#
# 2. Rankle, Master of Pranks  {2}{B}{B}
#    identity B · commander-legal · OTC 148 · Legendary Creature — Faerie Rogue
#    | Flying, haste
#    | Whenever Rankle deals combat damage to a player, choose any number —
#    | • Each player discards a card.
#    | • Each player loses 1 life and draws a card.
#    | • Each player sacrifices a creature of their choice.
#    The AGGRO reading of Tinybones: evasive body that forces the discard
#    itself. Symmetric, so it wants a hand the player can afford to empty.
#    67% inclusion in the Trinket Thief field, 66% in Bauble Burglar's.
#
# 3. The Haunt of Hightower  {4}{B}{B}
#    identity B · commander-legal · PLST RNA-273 · Legendary Creature — Vampire
#    | Flying, lifelink
#    | Whenever The Haunt of Hightower attacks, defending player discards a card.
#    | Whenever a card is put into an opponent's graveyard from anywhere, put a
#    | +1/+1 counter on The Haunt of Hightower.
#    VOLTRON reading: discard is the fuel, a flying lifelinker is the clock.
#    Simplest of the four to pilot; six mana is the cost.
#
# 4. Nath of the Gilt-Leaf  {3}{B}{G}
#    identity B G · commander-legal · C16 213 · Legendary Creature — Elf Warrior
#    | At the beginning of your upkeep, you may have target opponent discard a
#    | card at random.
#    | Whenever an opponent discards a card, you may create a 1/1 green Elf
#    | Warrior creature token.
#    The only analog that is BOTH engine and payoff in one card, and the only
#    one that goes wide. Costs the mono-black identity (so no Cabal Coffers /
#    Crypt Ghast) and adds green ramp.
#
# ALSO VERIFIED, and why each is NOT the answer:
#   Kroxa, Titan of Death's Hunger {B}{R} (THB 221) — "Whenever Kroxa enters or
#     attacks, each opponent discards a card, then each opponent who didn't
#     discard a nonland card this way loses 3 life." A recursive discard ENGINE,
#     but it sacrifices itself when cast from hand, so it is a poor commander
#     for a deck that wants the discard payoff to stay on the battlefield.
#   Lord Xander, the Collector {4}{U}{B}{R} (SNC 197) — discards half a hand on
#     ETB, but it is a one-shot at seven mana in three colours.
#   Sadistic Hypnotist {3}{B}{B} (DDJ 62) — "Sacrifice a creature: Target player
#     discards two cards." A support piece, not a commander (not legendary).
#   Maralen of the Mornsong {1}{B}{B} (MOR 65) — hand DENIAL, not discard; it
#     hands every opponent a tutor.
#   Nekusar, the Mindrazer {2}{U}{B}{R} (CMM 349) — draw-damage, not discard.
#   Braids, Arisen Nightmare {1}{B}{B} (EOC 82) — sacrifice punisher, adjacent.
#   Bone Miser {4}{B} (C19 15) — YOUR discard, and not legendary.
#   Chainer, Nightmare Adept {2}{B}{R} / Anje Falkenrath {1}{B}{R} — self-
#     discard / madness. Opposite direction of play.
#   Geth, Lord of the Vault {4}{B}{B} · Gonti, Canny Acquisitor {2}{B}{G}{U} —
#     graveyard/library THEFT, i.e. the Pickpocket half, no hand attack.
#   Ob Nixilis, Captive Kingpin {2}{B}{R} (MAT 41) — triggers on exactly 1 life
#     lost, not on discard. Verified because the name suggested otherwise.
#   Rankle and Torbran {1}{B}{B}{R}{R} (MOM 252) — the merged card DROPPED the
