# The Plan
Esper **Sphinx tribal**: 30 Sphinxes (29 printed plus Changeling Wayfinder). The Ur-Sphinx
does two jobs. From the command zone, its eminence makes every other Sphinx cost {1} less
before you ever cast it. On the battlefield, every Sphinx attack mills each player and
hands you a free spell from each of their piles. Make the Sphinxes cheap, turn each one
into cards, then land the Ur-Sphinx and attack in the air.

**Commander (verified):** {6}{W}{U}{B}, Legendary Creature — Sphinx Avatar.
*"Eminence — As long as The Ur-Sphinx is in the command zone or on the battlefield, other
Sphinx spells you cast cost {1} less to cast. Flying. Whenever one or more Sphinxes you
control attack, each player mills that many cards. For each player, you may cast a card
that player milled this way without paying its mana cost."*

**The rulings that matter:**
- You cast the free spells while the trigger resolves. You can't save them for later in
  the turn, but timing restrictions are ignored, so a milled sorcery like Rise of the
  Dark Realms is castable in combat.
- Any {X} is 0, so a milled Sphinx's Revelation does nothing.
- A Sphinx that enters attacking doesn't trigger it.

# How it wins
- **The discount stack.** The eminence is −1 on every other Sphinx, even from the command
  zone. The other three discounts apply to the Ur-Sphinx itself (eminence says "other"):
  - Unesh, Criosphinx Sovereign: −2 on Sphinx spells.
  - Warden of Evos Isle: −1 on creature spells with flying.
  - Defiler of Dreams: −{U} on blue permanents if you pay 2 life.

  The eminence, Unesh and Warden cut generic mana only; Defiler cuts one blue pip. With
  the eminence active and Unesh and Warden out, Consecrated Sphinx ({4}{U}{U}) costs
  {U}{U}, or {U} and 2 life with Defiler. Azor ({2}{W}{W}{U}{U}) bottoms out at
  {W}{W}{U}{U}, or {W}{W}{U} and 2 life.
- **Every Sphinx is a card:**
  - Unesh reveals four whenever it or another Sphinx enters. An opponent splits them into
    two piles; you take one and the other goes to your graveyard. Sphinx of Uthuun does the
    same with five as it enters, so with Unesh out it's two pile splits.
  - Defiler of Dreams draws on every blue permanent spell.
  - Windreader Sphinx draws whenever *any* creature with flying attacks, yours or theirs.
  - Consecrated Sphinx draws two whenever an opponent draws.
  - Rhystic Study taxes the table.
- **The attack.** With the Ur-Sphinx out, attack with N Sphinxes and each player mills N.
  Then you cast one card from each player's milled cards for free: up to four free spells
  a combat at a four-player table. More attackers means more to choose from, not more
  casts.
  - Yennett, Cryptic Sovereign adds a free cast from your own library on an odd mana
    value, else it draws. Enigma, Magister, Windreader and Uthuun, Scholar of the Lost
    Trove and Sphinx of the Final Word are 7s; Ethersworn Sphinx and Rise of the Dark
    Realms are 9s.
  - Azor, the Lawbringer and Elenda and Azor draw X when they attack, Raffine connives X,
    and Sphinx of Forgotten Lore gives a spell in your graveyard flashback.
- **The closers:**
  - Magister Sphinx sets a player's life to 10. It's mandatory, and a player below 10
    goes up to 10, so aim it at the leader.
  - Sphinx Sovereign makes each opponent lose 3 at your end step if it's tapped (it
    attacked). Untapped, you gain 3 instead.
  - Medomai the Ageless takes one extra turn. It can't attack in that turn, so it never
    chains.
  - Atemsis, All-Seeing makes an opponent it damages lose the game if you reveal six
    different mana values in hand, which Unesh's piles make realistic.
- **Rise of the Dark Realms is the reset button in reverse.** After a few attacks of mills,
  Unesh's discard piles and a sweeper, it puts every creature card in every graveyard onto
  the battlefield under your control.
- **The Mind Stone, once harnessed** ({5}{W}, T), blinks one of your nonland permanents at
  each of your end steps: Unesh for another pile, Magister Sphinx for another "life
  becomes 10", Sphinx of Enlightenment for another three cards. The card returns under
  its OWNER's control, so blink only your own cards, never a creature Rise of the Dark
  Realms took or one you cast from an opponent's mill.

# Mulligan for
- 3-4 lands with two colours, plus a two-mana rock or an early body: Warden of Evos Isle,
  Raffine, Sphinx of Foresight, Sphinx of Forgotten Lore, Curator of Mysteries or
  Changeling Wayfinder.
- You don't need the Ur-Sphinx early: the eminence works from the command zone.
- A hand with Unesh and the mana to cast it on turn 4-5 is the best keep in the deck.

# Sequencing tips
- **Unesh before the other big Sphinxes.** Every later Sphinx costs 3 less and draws a pile.
- **Warden of Evos Isle early.** A 3-drop that makes every later Sphinx, and the Ur-Sphinx
  itself, cost 1 less.
- **Cast the Ur-Sphinx when you can protect it.** It costs nine and draws every table's
  removal. Hold up Spectacular Tactics, Stone by Sunlight or a counterspell when you can.
- **Time Wipe returns ONE of your creatures to hand, then destroys the rest.** Usually
  that's Unesh: recast it for a fresh pile. Returning the Ur-Sphinx dodges commander tax,
  which applies only to casts from the command zone (CR 903.8). But its eminence is off
  while it sits in hand, and the sweep took the other discounters, so it costs the full
  {6}{W}{U}{B}.
- **Tragic Arrogance is yours to aim.** You choose the artifact, creature, enchantment and
  planeswalker each player keeps. Keep your best Sphinx and leave them their worst.
- **Cloud's Limit Break's Omnislash** ({1}{W} plus {3}{W}) during an opponent's combat
  destroys their tapped attackers. Your untapped Sphinxes are unaffected.
- **Hold Consecrated Sphinx until you can untap with it.** It's the deck's best draw engine
  and every table points removal at it.

# Weaknesses to respect
- **The mana.** Esper with no shocks or fetches: you own none of the field's Hallowed
  Fountain, Watery Grave, Godless Shrine or Raffine's Tower (buylist). Land sources are
  blue 23, white 17 and black 15, with Karsten targets of ~23 each. Arcane Signet, two
  Talismans, two Signets, Commander's Sphere, Wayfarer's Bauble, Chromatic Lantern and
  Patchwork Banner fix too. The
  WWUU cards (Azor, Isperia, Dream Trawler) are the hardest casts.
- **Slow.** Average mana value ~4.2, and a combat clock around turn 9. The discounts are
  what make it play like a 4-mana deck.
- **Thin protection.** Only the protect modes of Spectacular Tactics, Stone by Sunlight and
  Getaway Glamer. Sphinx of the Final Word makes your instants and sorceries uncounterable.
- **Graveyard hate** blanks Rise of the Dark Realms, Scholar of the Lost Trove and Sphinx
  of Forgotten Lore.

Kwia Vigorbloom is green-white, so 33 are in Esper colours.
- **The build:** the engine spec (`the-ur-sphinx.engine.json`) seeded 20 cards before any
  quota. Every other non-land card has a keep reason, or the field's case at 10%+ of
  decks.
- **The field:** 5,253 EDHREC decks. Owned top-25 coverage is 20 of 21. The one out is Path
  of Ancestry: all seven copies are in other decks.
- **Bracket 3:**
  - 2 Game Changers. Consecrated Sphinx is pinned here. Rhystic Study is the third copy,
    allocated by the player to the Ur-Dragon, Y'shtola and this deck.
  - One extra-turn card (Medomai).
  - No combos: Commander Spellbook finds 0 present.

    Uthuun.
  - Inspired Sphinx: out for Sphinx of Uthuun, which arrived with the player's master list
