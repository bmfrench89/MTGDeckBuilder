# Thorin, King of Durin's Folk — "The Company and the Fire"

Built 2026-10-15 by a session at the player's request ("build my dwarves deck … I like the
idea of dwarves and b list red/white dragons that aren't being used by ur dragon"). An
auto_build draft over the free pool, then a hand pass. **Every free Dwarf in the
collection is here**, plus two changelings and a B-list of six red-white Dragons the
Ur-Dragon doesn't use. Every card is a free copy. Where Thorin and one of the session's own
decks (Tifa, Sephiroth) wanted the same single copy, Thorin kept it, except Diamond
Pick-Axe, Rogue's Passage and Slayers' Stronghold, which were cheap to replace.

## The engine

**Verified commander text** (Scryfall, 2026-10-15):

> Thorin, King of Durin's Folk — {3}{R}{W} — Legendary Creature — Dwarf Noble — 4/4
> Whenever Thorin or another Dwarf you control enters, create a Treasure token.
> Other Dwarves you control get +1/+0 for each artifact token you control.

**Every Dwarf is a Treasure, and every Treasure is +1/+0 on every other Dwarf.**

- **Dwarves that bring more Dwarves:**
  - Fíli the Pathfinder: whenever a nontoken Dwarf enters, make a 2/2 Dwarf token. That's
    a second Thorin trigger.
  - An Unexpected Party's adventure, At the Door: X 2/2 Dwarf tokens. Then cast the
    enchantment from exile: Dwarves +2/+2.
  - Dwarven Shortsword and The Lonely Mountain make Dwarf tokens.
  - Feisty Spikeling and Flock Impostor are changelings, so they're Dwarves too.
  - Flock Impostor (flash) also bounces a Dwarf to replay it for another Treasure.
- **Bifur, Melodic Rider doubles the deck.** With an enduring story (three artifacts,
  legendaries and/or Sagas, which Treasures cover), "if a triggered ability of a Dwarf you
  control triggers, that ability triggers an additional time". Bifur's own Storied
  counts toward the story. Thorin is a Dwarf, so every Dwarf makes **two** Treasures.
  Fíli makes two tokens and Balin triggers twice. Kíli's draw says "only once each turn",
  so don't count on a second Kíli draw.
- **Card flow off the same entries:**
  - Kíli: a Dwarf or Equipment enters, draw (once a turn).
  - Giott: loot on each Dwarf or Equipment entering.
  - Tocasia's Welcome: a creature with mana value 3 or less enters, draw (once a turn).
  - Balin, Loremaster: wheel your hand, and with a story, deal that much to each opponent.
  - The Arkenstone draws at your end step. Its adventure, Seek the Heart, tutors Bifur or
    Fíli.
- **Equipment** (Kíli makes the first equip each turn free once you have a story):
  - Dwarven Mattock, Bloodforged Battle-Axe (copies itself on damage, and the copies are
    artifact tokens), Long-Lost Lances (first strike), Orcrist, The Reaver Cleaver,
    Sting, Bilbo's Sword.
  - Iron Hills Blacksmith and Dáin Ironfoot make Axe tokens, which are also artifact
    tokens. When Dáin Ironfoot attacks, each equipped attacker gains double strike.

### The dragon B-list
- **Draconic Visitor** is the second engine. "If one or more artifact tokens would be
  created under your control, that many 5/5 red Dragon creature tokens with flying are
  created instead."
  - Every Thorin Treasure becomes a 5/5 flier: each Dwarf brings a dragon, and with Bifur,
    two.
  - It's not optional, and it also replaces Axe tokens, Battle-Axe copies, Orcrist's and
    The Reaver Cleaver's Treasures, and Smaug's upkeep Treasure. So you trade mana and
    Thorin's +1/+0 for 5/5 fliers. Cast it when you're ready to win, not to ramp.
  - The Dragons aren't Dwarves, so there's no loop.
  - **Never add Maskwood Nexus** (owned, deliberately left out). It makes every creature a
    Dwarf, so each Dragon token would trigger Thorin again: a mandatory infinite loop,
    which CR 104.4b (retrieved) makes a draw.
- **Smaug the Magnificent:** a Treasure each upkeep. Each attack deals damage equal to
  your Treasures to any target.
- **Parapet Thrasher:** whenever your Dragons connect, destroy an artifact, deal 4 to each
  other opponent, or impulse-draw. Visitor's tokens are Dragons.
- **Hellkite Tyrant:** steals artifacts on hit, and wins at 20 artifacts in your upkeep.
  Treasures count.
- **Atsushi** (dies: three Treasures, or exile your top two cards to play until the end of
  your next turn), **Lorehold, the Historian** (haste, and on each opponent's upkeep you
  may discard a card to draw one), and **The Misty Mountains Cold** (a Treasure per
  chapter, then a 6/6 Dragon at four Treasures).

## Interaction
- **Removal:** Swords to Plowshares, Chaos Warp, White Auracite, Web Up, Solitary Cell and
  Celebrate the Mountain-king (one nonland permanent from *each* opponent).
- **Artifact and enchantment removal:** Thorin's Last Stand, Shattered Acolyte.
- **Sweepers:**
  - Settle the Wreckage (exile their attackers).
  - Glóin's Easy Pickings (1 to each of their creatures).
- **Blasphemous Act with Hofri Ghostforge out:** your nontoken creatures come back as
  Spirit token copies.
  - Hofri's trigger is a leaves-the-battlefield ability, so it sees the creatures that die
    with it (CR 603.10a, retrieved).
  - If Hofri dies too, the copies lose his +1/+1, trample and haste, so they can't attack
    that turn. Cast Bofur's Concerted Care on Hofri first (indestructible) and he survives
    the Act.
  - Leave Thorin in the graveyard rather than the command zone (CR 903.9a makes that the
    owner's choice). Resolve his copy first, and every Dwarf copy after it makes a
    Treasure.
- **Protection:**
  - Bofur's Concerted Care: hexproof and indestructible.
  - Dwarven Mattock: ward {1}. Thorin Oakenshield, with an enduring story: ward {1} on all
    your artifacts and creatures.
  - Bilbo's Gambit: bounce a spell. Promise the gift and no one casts spells for the rest
    of the turn.
  - The Eagles Are Coming!: kicked, it saves the board from a wrath, returns 4/4 fliers and
    lets you replay the Dwarves for Treasures.
  - Dáin, Lord of the Iron Hills, with an enduring story: attacks on you cost {1} each.

## Pilot notes
- **Turns 1-3:** play cheap Dwarves and rocks. Thorin on turn 5 (goldfish: 47% by turn 5,
  mean 5.65), or earlier off Dori, Glóin or Treasures.
- **After Thorin:** count artifact tokens before combat; each one is +1/+0 on every other
  Dwarf.
- **The two plans don't mix:** go wide with Treasures, or turn on Visitor. Visitor ends
  the Treasure plan.
- **Finishers:** Secret Tunnel makes two Dwarves unblockable, and Smaug's attack trigger
  scales with your Treasure count.

## The numbers (2026-10-15)
- **Bracket 2** (the player's call, 2026-10-15): no Game Changers, tutors or combos. The only
  Game Changer you own in red-white is Enlightened Tutor, and it's Giada's.
  It plays at the top of Bracket 2: a doubled engine, 9 interaction, 12 draw.
- **Sram went to the Eriette deck (2026-10-15).** The player gave that list priority. Dwarven
  Provisioner took the slot: another 2-mana Dwarf (a Treasure on entry), and {3}{W} gives
  the team +1/+1 for a turn, a sink for spare Treasures.
- **Field:** 20 of EDHREC's top 25 for this Thorin (7,870 decks). None of the other 5 are
  owned: Fíli and Kíli, Joyous; Glóin, Dwarf Emissary; Magda, Brazen Outlaw; Ori, Plate
  Stacker; Gimli of the Glittering Caves. They're the buy list.
- **Optimizer read:** it proposed Draconic Visitor → Boros Charm (Ur-Dragon's copy) and
  Lorehold → Gleaming Splendor (Giada's copy). Both rejected: Visitor is the second
  engine and Lorehold is on the player's dragon list.
- **Mana:** 36 lands (9 Mountain, 9 Plains), R 26 / W 25 sources, keepable 79%.
- **Goldfish:** combat-only lethal median turn 9. It models no Treasures, no Dwarf pumps
  and no Dragon tokens, so read it as a floor.
