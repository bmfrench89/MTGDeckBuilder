# Sephiroth, Fallen Hero — "One-Winged Angel"

Built 2026-10-14 by a session at the player's request ("be creative … create 2 brand new
decks from cards I own … bracket 3/4"). Every card is a FREE copy, so there are no conflicts
with any other deck, Tifa included. It was built second, from what Tifa left.

## The engine

**Verified commander text** (Scryfall, 2026-10-14):

> Sephiroth, Fallen Hero — {3}{R}{W} — Legendary Creature — Human Avatar Soldier — 7/5
> Jenova Cells — Whenever Sephiroth attacks, you may put a cell counter on target creature.
> Until end of turn, each modified creature you control has base power and toughness 7/5.
> (Equipment, Auras you control, and counters are modifications.)
> The Reunion — {3}, Sacrifice a modified creature: Return this card from your graveyard to
> the battlefield tapped.

**Modify everything, then attack: every modified creature is a 7/5.** Sephiroth sets
*base* power and toughness. Equipment and Aura bonuses still apply on top, so a Samurai's
Katana Hero becomes a 9/7 with trample and haste.
- **Bodies that arrive modified:**
  - Job-select Equipment: "create a 1/1 colorless Hero creature token, then attach this to
    it". Samurai's Katana, Dragoon's Lance (flying on your turn), Paladin's Arms, White
    Mage's Staff, Warrior's Sword, Dancer's Chakrams (also gives Sephiroth +2/+2 and
    lifelink).
  - Living weapon: Bitterthorn.
  - Zack Fair enters with a counter, Spider-Punk has riot, and Guardian Scalelord's backup
    can target itself.
- **Mass modification:**
  - The Crystal's Chosen: four Heroes, then a +1/+1 counter on each creature you control.
  - Requisition Raid and Collective Effort: a counter on each creature target player
    controls.
  - Duty Beyond Death: a counter on each, plus indestructible.
  - SOLDIER Military Program: a Soldier each combat, and with a commander out, counters on
    two Soldiers.
  - Rosa: a counter each combat.
  - Elspeth Resplendent's +1.
- **Doubling:** Windcrag Siege (Mardu mode): "If a creature attacking causes a triggered
  ability of a permanent you control to trigger, that ability triggers an additional time."
  - Sephiroth places two cell counters.
  - Guardian Scalelord returns two permanents.
  - Diamond Pick-Axe makes two Treasures.
  - **Scalelord's limit:** its target is chosen as the trigger goes on the stack (CR 603.3d
    and 601.2c, retrieved), before Jenova Cells resolves. So X is Scalelord's power *then*:
    4 with its own backup counter, not 7. (Corrected 2026-10-14; the storyboard's read
    caught it.)
  - **Unconfirmed:** whether "whenever you attack" triggers (Adeline, Champions from
    Beyond's Light Party) count as "a creature attacking" for Windcrag Siege. Scryfall has
    no rulings for the Siege yet, so don't count on those doublings.
- **Kill:** True Conviction (double strike and lifelink for everything), Fireshrieker,
  Brass Knuckles (cast it: two copies, so double strike with both attached). An army of
  7/5 double strikers.
- **Engine and recursion:**
  - Puresteel Paladin: Equipment draws a card, and with metalcraft, equip {0}.
  - Sram: Aura and Equipment spells draw.
  - Danitha and Cid: cost reduction. Codsworth: attaches.
  - Forge Anew: equip at instant speed.
  - Skullclamp turns 1/1 Heroes into two cards.
  - The Reunion brings Sephiroth back by sacrificing a modified token.

## Interaction
- Removal: Swords to Plowshares, Path to Exile, Chaos Warp, Abrade, Rip Apart, Sheltered by
  Ghosts (an Aura on your creature that exiles theirs, and also modifies yours), Planar
  Disruption, Hanged Executioner.
- Protection: Moogles' Valor, Grand Crescendo, Duty Beyond Death, Take Up the Shield,
  Swiftfoot Boots, Bastion Protector (commander +2/+2 and indestructible).

## Pilot notes
- Turn 2-3: a job-select Equipment or Zack Fair. Turn 5: Sephiroth (goldfish mean 5.75).
- Before the attack, count modified creatures: each one becomes a 7/5. Use the cell
  counter on the best unmodified body, or on an opponent's creature only when you have to.
- Hold Moogles' Valor or Grand Crescendo for the wrath. A modified token is fuel for The
  Reunion.

## The numbers (2026-10-14)
- **Bracket 3, declared** (the player's request). The detector reads **2**: there are no
  Game Changers in red-white that the collection holds free. The tuning is Upgraded: a
  doubled attack engine, 9 interaction, and True Conviction.
- **Field:** 16 of EDHREC's top 25 for Sephiroth (3,715 decks). Of the other 9, Tifa
  holds Lightning Greaves and Vincent, other decks hold Boros Charm and Talisman of
  Conviction, and 4 aren't owned.
- **Optimizer read:** it proposed cutting True Conviction, both Elspeths, The Crystal's
  Chosen and Guardian Scalelord for field cards that other decks hold. All rejected. Dancer's
  Chakrams (46% field, verified job-select) went in over Adelbert Steiner on the read.
- **Mana:** 36 lands (4 Mountain, 11 Plains). W 25 sources for heavy demand, R 18 for light
  demand. Keepable 80%.
- **Goldfish:** 44% lethal by turn 8, median 9. It doesn't model the 7/5 conversion, so
  read it as a floor.
