# Doctor Doom — All According to Plan
## Game plan
Mono-black grind. Doom arrives with two 3/3 Doombots, stays **indestructible while you
control any artifact creature or a Plan**, and draws you a card every end step for 1
life. Wipe the board freely — Doom survives, the Doombots rebuild, and the card
advantage never stops. Villain bodies + robot chaff hold the ground; drain closes.

**Commander (verified 2026-08-15, MSH 95):** {4}{B}{B} Legendary Human Scientist
Villain. NOT the Grixis "Doctor Doom, Rules All" — this is the mono-B one we own.

## Engine pieces (do not cut)
- **Castle Doom** — artifact-only rainbow mana + a Doombot factory on a LAND.
- **Doom Reigns Supreme** — the Plan: keeps Doom indestructible by itself, drains per
  Villain ETB, and its fifth counter casts two free spells off an opponent's library.
- **Robot/Construct density** — every artifact creature is a live indestructibility
  switch for Doom. Don't trade them all away cheaply.
- **Extinction Event honesty note:** it EXILES — fine here (our own yard barely
  matters), and Doom himself just survives via indestructible? No — exile beats
  indestructible; cast it only when Doom is protected or replayable. Say this at
  the table, not after.

## Mulligan for
Three lands and any 2-drop artifact creature. Doom on 6 with the switch already on
is the whole opening plan.

## Round-4 verified keeps (do not cut)
- **The Masters of Evil** — Villain anthem AND a tutor for Plan cards: it finds Doom
  Reigns Supreme, which alone keeps Doom indestructible. Engine, not filler.
- **Madame Masque** — connive + a Villain token on the second draw each turn, and
  Doom's own end-step draw turns her on reliably.
