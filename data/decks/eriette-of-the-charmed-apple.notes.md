# The Plan
White-black **Auras**, built 2026-10-15 from the player's own deck list (75 cards bought for
it). The deck has two jobs:
- **The table's creatures:** goad them into each other, shrink them, lock them down.
- **One threat of your own:** stack Auras on it.

Eriette makes every creature wearing one of your Auras unable to attack you. At your end
step she drains each opponent for the number of Auras you control.

**Commander (verified):** {1}{W}{B}, Legendary Creature — Human Warlock.
*"Each creature that's enchanted by an Aura you control can't attack you or planeswalkers
you control. At the beginning of your end step, each opponent loses X life and you gain X
life, where X is the number of Auras you control."*

Killian, Decisive Mentor was the other commander option and plays in the 99. Eriette won on
the field: 22 of her EDHREC top 25 were free in the collection, against 18 of Killian's, and
Killian's own field runs Eriette in the 99.

# How it wins
- **The drain.** Each Aura you control drains each opponent for 1 at each of your end steps.
  Auras on opponents' creatures count: with six Auras out, each opponent loses six on every
  one of your turns.
- **Goad turns their creatures on each other.** A goaded creature attacks each combat, and
  attacks a player other than you if able. Eriette makes attacking you impossible anyway.
  - The goad Auras: Parasitic, Coercive, Ghoulish and Martial Impetus, and Redemption Arc.
  - Killian, Decisive Mentor goads a creature whenever an enchantment of yours enters. He
    also draws you a card whenever creatures wearing your Auras attack, on their turns too.
  - Coercive Impetus draws as its creature attacks, and you lose 1 life. Parasitic Impetus
    makes that creature's controller lose 2 and you gain 2.
- **One big threat.**
  - Kor Spiritdancer gets +2/+2 per Aura on it and draws when you cast an Aura.
  - Ethereal Armor and All That Glitters scale with your enchantments.
  - Angelic Destiny adds +4/+4, flying and first strike. Eidolon of Countless Battles counts
    creatures and Auras. Spirit Mantle gives protection from creatures.
  - Swiftfoot Boots adds hexproof and haste, and Rogue's Passage makes it unblockable.
- **Card flow.**
  - Mesa Enchantress draws on any enchantment spell, and Sram, Senior Edificer on any Aura
    spell. With Kor Spiritdancer out too, one Aura is three cards.
  - Light-Paws, Emperor's Voice: whenever you cast an Aura, it fetches a cheaper Aura with a
    different name from your library and attaches it to Light-Paws.
  - Sage's Reverie draws as it enters, one card per Aura you control on a creature, theirs
    included.
  - Hateful Eidolon draws when any enchanted creature dies, one card per Aura you controlled
    on it. Ashiok's Reaper draws when one of your enchantments goes to the graveyard.
  - Angelic Gift and Rune of Mortality each replace themselves.
- **Grievous Wound** goes on a PLAYER. They can't gain life, and every time they're dealt
  damage they lose half their life, rounded up. Goaded attackers are happy to deal it.
- **Winds of Rath** destroys every creature that isn't enchanted. Your threat survives, and
  so do their goaded creatures, which still can't attack you.
- **Rebuilding.**
  - Retether returns every Aura card from your graveyard onto creatures.
  - Eiganjo Dynastorian becomes prepared when you attack with two or more creatures. Its
    prepared spell, Replenish, returns every enchantment card from your graveyard.
  - Neva, Kaya's Ghostform and Gift of Immortality bring creatures back.
- **A flying board.** Sigil of the Empty Throne makes a 4/4 flying Angel per enchantment
  spell. Archon of Sun's Grace makes a 2/2 Pegasus per enchantment that enters.
- **Ghostly Prison** charges {2} per creature that attacks you, on top of Eriette's lock on
  the enchanted ones.

# The cost reducers
- **Killian, Ink Duelist: −2 on spells that target a creature.**
  - Aura spells cast on a creature qualify.
  - Grievous Wound enchants a player and gets nothing.
  - Planar Disruption, Clawing Torment and Rune of Mortality get it only when cast on a
    creature.
- **The rest:**
  - Transcendent Envoy and Hero of Iroas: −1 on Aura spells.
  - Danitha Capashen, Paragon: −1 on Auras and Equipment.
  - Starfield Mystic: −1 on enchantment spells.
  - Pearl-Ear, Imperial Advisor: affinity for Auras on enchantment spells.
  - Codsworth, Handy Helper: taps for {W}{W} toward Auras, and gives your commander ward {2}.

They reduce only generic mana. The checker counts 6 ramp, which is just the rocks; the
reducers are the deck's real ramp.

  Spiritdancer, Sram), plus cheap Auras.
- Eriette on turn 3 is the plan; she doesn't need to attack.

# Sequencing tips
- **Goad the biggest creature at the table.** It must attack someone else every turn, and
  every creature wearing your Aura is one more point of drain.
- **Removal is mostly Auras.**
  - Darksteel Mutation turns a creature, even a commander, into an indestructible 0/1
    artifact Insect with no other abilities.
  - Planar Disruption stops attacking, blocking and activated abilities.
  - Stab Wound and Clawing Torment shrink and drain.
  - The hard answers are Swords to Plowshares, Path to Exile, Anguished Unmaking and
    Sheltered by Ghosts.
- **Changing Loyalty and Minion's Return have flash.** Put one on an opponent's best
  creature; when it dies, it comes back under your control.
- **Before Winds of Rath,** make sure your threat is enchanted.
- **Protect the threat** with Swiftfoot Boots, Timely Ward (flash when it targets a
  commander), Hyena Umbra or Not Dead After All.

# Weaknesses to respect
- **Enchantment removal and sacrifice.** When an enchanted creature leaves, its Auras go too.
  - Hyena Umbra (umbra armor) and Timely Ward (indestructible) stop destruction only, not
    sacrifice, exile or bounce.
  - Kaya's Ghostform and Gift of Immortality bring the creature back.
  - Retether and Eiganjo's Replenish bring the Auras back.
- **Aura removal can be undone.** A sacrifice outlet or a bounce frees the creature, and a
  commander under Darksteel Mutation can be sacrificed and recast fresh from the command
  zone. Save the hard answers for those.
- **Bracket 2 (Core):** no Game Changers.
- **Never add Bloodletter of Aclazotz, Wound Reflection, Archfiend of Despair or Warlock
  Class.** Commander Spellbook lists each with Grievous Wound as a two-card win.
