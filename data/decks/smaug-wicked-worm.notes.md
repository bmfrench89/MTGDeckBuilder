# Smaug, Wicked Worm — Dragon's Hoard

## Game plan
Rakdos treasure aristocrats. **Smaug, Wicked Worm** (HOB 281, player-verified 2026-08-15):
his ETB makes Treasures equal to opponents' artifacts (**they enter TAPPED** — runner-
verified verbatim, so the hoard comes online the turn after he lands), and any spell cast
with Treasure mana draws a card (lose 1) — so the hoard IS the draw engine. Bank Treasures, spend
them in bursts, and kill with scaled burn or the artifact alt-win.

## Engine pieces (do not cut)
- **Smaug the Magnificent** — upkeep Treasure + attack trigger: damage equal to Treasures
  you control, any target. The hoard is the burn spell. (75% of the field runs him here.)
- **The Reaver Cleaver** — combat damage → that many Treasures. On either Smaug the
  hoard snowballs.
- **Hellkite Tyrant** — the alt-win: 20+ artifacts at upkeep wins, **and Treasures are
  artifacts**, so the commander's own ETB can arm it in one swing. Also steals a whole
  artifact board on combat damage.
- **The Sackville-Bagginses** — "Whenever you sacrifice a token, target opponent loses
  1 life": every Treasure spent becomes drain. The aristocrats half of the deck.
- **Professional Face-Breaker** — Treasure per combat + Treasures cash into impulse draw.
- **Blasphemous Act** — near-free wipe in a deck that goes wide with fodder; see the
  Impenetrable line below.
- **Deadly Dispute / Big Score / Seize the Spoils** — draw that MAKES Treasures.
- **Dark Ritual** — ⚠ owned x1 and shared with yshtola-nights-blessed (dashboard badges
  it; wishlist tracks the extra copy).

## The line the table won't see coming (needs one physical decision)
**Smaug the Impenetrable + Blasphemous Act**: he is indestructible and turns every point
of noncombat damage into a Treasure — your own 13-damage wipe clears the board, keeps the
8/7, and banks **13 Treasures**. The copy is currently **sleeved in The Ur-Dragon** (own
x1), so either pull it (Ur-Dragon runs 30+ dragons; it's a swap, not a hole) or wishlist
a second copy. Until then the deck plays fine without it — the line is listed here, not
in the 99.

## Pilot notes
- Sequence the commander AFTER opponents deploy rocks/equipment — his ETB counts their
  artifacts at that moment.
- Hellkite Tyrant's win check is at YOUR upkeep: bank Treasures the turn before, don't
  spend into it.
- Spending a Treasure = drain 1 with Sackville-Bagginses out — cash the whole hoard in
  response to removal.
- Smaug the Magnificent wants the hoard UNSPENT on the attack — the two Smaugs disagree
  about saving vs spending; the Magnificent's trigger counts on attack, so swing first,
  spend second.
- No counterspells (Rakdos): the protection is speed and redundancy, not permission.
