# The Plan
Five-color **dragons**. The Ur-Dragon discounts every dragon you cast from the command
zone, and once dragons attack you refill your hand *and* cheat a permanent into play.
Ramp hard, land dragons ahead of curve, swing, draw, repeat.

**Commander (verified):** {4}{W}{U}{B}{R}{G}, Legendary Creature — Dragon Avatar.
- *Eminence* — while Ur-Dragon is **in the command zone** or on the battlefield, your other
  Dragon spells cost **{1} less**. (You get the discount without ever casting him — important,
  because he's a 9-drop.)
- Flying.
- *Whenever one or more Dragons you control attack, **draw that many cards**, then you may
  put a permanent card from your hand onto the battlefield.*

# How it wins
- **Eminence from turn one.** The discount is active from the command zone, so your dragons
  are effectively a mana cheaper all game — you never need to hard-cast the 9-drop.
- **The attack trigger is the engine.** Even two dragons attacking draws 2 and free-drops a
  permanent (ideally another dragon, or a land if you're stuck).
- **The dragons you own are individually strong:** Atsushi (value on death), Ao (value on
  death), Hraesvelgr, Silumgar (deathtouch tax), Niv-Mizzet, Beledros, Fin Fang Foom.
- **Five-color fixing + ramp** (Sol Ring, signets, Rampant Growth, Wild Growth, Priest of
  Titania, Grow from the Ashes) is what makes a WUBRG pile castable.

# Mulligan for
- **Fixing above all.** 2–3 lands that produce more than one color, or a mana rock. A
  five-color deck that stumbles on colors does nothing.
- A ramp piece is better than a dragon in the opener — you want turn-4/5 dragons, not a
  dragon stuck in hand.

# Sequencing tips
- Ramp on 2–3, dragon on 4–5. Don't hold dragons for "the big turn" — the attack trigger
  only pays you if they're on the battlefield.
- With the attack trigger, **attack with more dragons rather than fewer** — the card draw
  usually outweighs a bad block, and the free permanent drop compounds.
- Hold Swords to Plowshares / Vendetta / Rip Apart for the table's actual threat; you have
  enough bodies that you don't need to trade early.

# Weaknesses to respect
- **The tribe is shallow.** You own ~13 dragons and 2 are committed to other decks, so this
  runs **10** — the eminence discount and the attack trigger both scale with dragon count,
  and neither is firing at full power yet. See `the-ur-dragon.buylist.csv`.
- **~16 creature slots are vanilla filler** (Raging Goblin, Cloud Sprite, Merfolk of the
  Pearl Trident, Goblin Recruiter…). They're there because the owned dragon pool ran dry —
  they do **not** trigger Ur-Dragon and they don't advance the plan. They're the first cards
  to cut, either for bought dragons or for more ramp / removal / card draw you already own.
- **Five colors on a mostly-basic base.** Mana is the real constraint; prioritize duals.
- **No Dragon tribal payoffs yet** (no Dragon Tempest, Scourge of Valkas, Temur Ascendancy,
  Sarkhan effects) — the deck is "some dragons," not yet "a dragon deck."
