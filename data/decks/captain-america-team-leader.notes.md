# The Plan
Jeskai **Hero tribal**. Captain America turns every Hero you play into an immediate
threat: each one enters with vigilance + haste, gets a **+1/+1 counter**, and puts a
counter on Cap too. Play cheap Heroes, attack every turn, and let Cap snowball while
your counterspells and removal keep the table honest.

**Commander (verified):** {R}{W}{U}, Legendary Creature — Human Soldier Hero.
*"Whenever another Hero you control enters, it gains vigilance and haste until end of
turn. Put a +1/+1 counter on that Hero and a +1/+1 counter on Captain America."*

# How it wins
- **Cap grows every turn.** Each Hero ETB is a counter on Cap — he outgrows removal
  ranges fast and threatens 21 commander damage in three or four connections.
- **Free haste + vigilance** means every Hero you deploy attacks the turn it lands and
  still blocks. There's no summoning-sick turn in this deck.
- **The Hero base is wide and cheap** — Masked Meower, Speed, Misty Knight, Quicksilver,
  Iron Fist, Kid Loki, Hawkeye, Spider-Gwen, The Wasp — so you can rebuild after a wipe.
- **The control shell protects the clock:** Counterspell, Force of Will, Essence Scatter,
  Memory Lapse, plus Swords-style removal (Dispatch, Condemn, Rip Apart, Abrade).

# Mulligan for
- 2–3 lands, a mana rock or a 1–2 mana Hero, and ideally a piece of interaction.
- You do **not** need Cap in the opener — he's the payoff. Deploy Heroes first, then land
  Cap into a board that's ready to attack.
- Blue is the tightest color (Counterspell/Force of Will want UU) — keep hands with a
  blue source if you're holding counters.

# Sequencing tips
- **Hold Heroes until Cap is out** when you can afford to. A Hero played before Cap gets
  no counter and no haste — the same card is dramatically better one turn later.
- Attack with everything Cap has grown; vigilance means you keep blockers up anyway.
- Save counterspells for wraths and the table's engine, not for the first threat you see.
  This deck rebuilds from a wipe better than most, but Cap himself is the exception.
- Mystic Remora carries the long game — deploy it on an off-turn when you can still hold
  up an answer. SP//dr, Piloted by Peni is the other draw engine: every Hero that entered
  under Cap carries a +1/+1 counter, so each one that connects draws a card.

# Weaknesses to respect
- **Cap is the deck.** Once he's grown, he eats removal. Play around one wrath; don't
  over-commit the whole hand into an open blue table.
- **Blue sources are thin** relative to the double-blue spells — this is the manabase's
  weak point. Prioritize the blue duals/fixing when you draw them.
- **Counters vanish with the creature.** Unlike anthems, the +1/+1 counters are on the
  bodies — a sweeper resets your investment, so keep a protective answer up.

## Deep-research pass (2026-08-11) — confirmations & gotchas
- All four audit swaps CONFIRMED by community research (field snapshot + precon
  upgrade guides + rules sources; see git log for URLs).
- Thor, Asgard's Avenger added over Think Twice (0% field): 45% field, own 2,
  was in no deck. On-tribe, himself worthy (backup Mjölnir carrier), and his
  +1 damage amp turns Mjölnir's ETB into 5.

  spell that turn. Their spell is your permission slip, not free flash.
