

## 2026-10-13 refresh — best build from the collection

Ur-Dragon and Thorin study builds. Texts verified on deck-verify run 38456497906. Seven free
field cards came in:
- **Angel of the Ruins** (37%).
- **Archaeomancer's Map** (31%), which keeps a mono-white deck on land parity.
- **Flare of Fortitude** (30%), which can be cast free by sacrificing a white creature, for
  team hexproof and indestructible.
- **Serra the Benevolent** (30%), whose −3 is an immediate 4/4 Angel.
- **Ob Nixilis, the Ascended** (28%), a 7-mana Angel. On entry it destroys every tapped
  opposing creature, and it makes a 4/4 Angel at each end step in which you gained life.
- **The Wind Crystal** (27%).
- **Cleansing Nova** (27%).
