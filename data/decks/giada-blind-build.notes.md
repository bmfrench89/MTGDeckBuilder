# Giada, Font of Hope — blind build (study copy)

Claude's build from 2026-10-12, made without seeing the player's Giada list so the two can
be compared. This is a study copy: it reserves no cards. Once the player's real Giada list
arrives, it becomes `giada-font-of-hope`.

## Engine

Giada makes every other Angel enter with +1/+1 counters, one per Angel already in play.
Angel tokens count too.
- **Court of Grace** makes a 4/4 Angel every upkeep while you are the monarch.
- **The Book of Exalted Deeds** and **Angelic Accord** make Angels from lifegain, which
  Lyra (angels gain lifelink), Righteous Valkyrie and Serra Ascendant supply.
- **Gleaming Splendor** is mono-white card draw. Activate it on an opponent's turn after
  their draw step: you draw and get a Treasure.

## Protection and finishers

- **Akroma's Will** and **Flawless Maneuver** (free while Giada is out).
- **Galadriel's Dismissal**, which kicked phases out your whole board and so dodges any wipe.
- **Lightning Greaves** and **Swiftfoot Boots**.

## Numbers

- 100 cards, all owned.
- 31 Angels, 37 lands (21 Plains).
- 19 of the field's top 25. The other 6 are unowned: Herald of War, Starnheim Aspirant,
  Resplendent Angel, Archangel of Thune, Herald's Horn, Emeria Shepherd.
- Bracket 2: no Game Changers.

## Player catches

Galadriel's Dismissal and Gleaming Splendor (each in 8% of Giada decks) were added after
the player pointed them out. The hand pass had started from the field's top cards, so it
missed these low-% fits.
