# Cloud, Ex-SOLDIER — Omnislash

## Game plan
Naya voltron: suit Cloud up cheap (his own attach discount), swing huge, kill via
commander damage. The one-shot assembly: Colossus Hammer attached free by Cloud plus
Fireshrieker or Boros Charm's double strike — 21+ commander damage in one connect.
Godo, Bandit Warlord is the second tutor line: he fetches any Equipment straight onto
the battlefield and grants an extra combat every turn he attacks. Crop Rotation
fetches Slayers' Stronghold at instant speed (the free owned Game Changer that holds
this deck at Bracket 3).

## Engine pieces (do not cut — the 2026-08-11 optimizer pass cut several of these
## for field-popular FF cards; the field builds Cloud precon-adjacent, this list does not)
- Colossus Hammer — the weapon. Free attach skips the equip 8.
- Fireshrieker — double strike turns one connect into the kill.
- Godo, Bandit Warlord — equipment tutor onto the battlefield + extra combat.
- True Conviction — team double strike + lifelink; redundant kill enabler.
- Crop Rotation — instant-speed Slayers' Stronghold; the Game Changer.
- All That Glitters — scales with the artifact count; second Hammer some games.
- Gift of Immortality — Cloud dies, returns, re-suits free on the next attack.
- Boros Charm — protection AND a kill mode; never cut.
- Puresteel Paladin / Sram, Senior Edificer / Skullclamp — the draw engines.
- Tireless Tracker — green's card flow; clues keep the hand full mid-game.
- Bastion Protector / Clever Concealment / Heroic Intervention — commander protection.

## Pilot notes
Mulligan for: 2-3 lands + a cheap equipment or Puresteel/Sram. Sequence: rock/dork
T1-2, Cloud T3, suit up T4, protect the swing. Against wipes: bank Boros Charm /
Clever Concealment; Gift of Immortality is the insurance policy. Godo lines: with
Fireshrieker already down he threatens 24 commander-adjacent damage alone across his
double combat.
