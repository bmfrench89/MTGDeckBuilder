

## 2026-10-13 refresh — best build from the collection

Rebuilt after the binder and Reality Fracture arrivals, as a set with the Giada and Thorin
study builds (no card used past its owned count). Texts verified on deck-verify run 38456497906.
- **Birds of Paradise** (37% field) in for Wood Elves. A one-mana source of any colour beats
  a four-mana Forest.
- **Beast Within** (12%) in for Smaug the Impenetrable. Removal was the thinnest role.
- **Scavenger Regent // Exude Toxin** (18%) in for Smaug the Magnificent. It is a 4-mana
  Dragon (3 with eminence) with ward. Its Omen side gives every non-Dragon -X/-X, so it is a
  one-sided wipe in this deck. Smaug went to the Thorin build, where the field plays it at 58%.
- **Rockfall Vale, Haunted Ridge, Overgrown Farmland** in for 2 Mountains and the Plains.
  Colour sources: B +1, G +2, the rest unchanged.
- **Field:** 21 of the top 25. The other four aren't owned.
- **Shared, not solved:** Kindred Discovery, Riverpyre Verge and Secluded Courtyard are also
  in Captain America's list, and Twilight Mire is in Dina's. One copy is owned of each. The
  logged decks already share them, so this build flags them rather than guessing.
