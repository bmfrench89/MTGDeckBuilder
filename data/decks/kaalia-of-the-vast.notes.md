# Kaalia of the Vast — Open the Gates
## Game plan
Mardu cheat-the-gates. Kaalia's attack drops an Angel, Demon, or Dragon from hand onto
the battlefield tapped and attacking — every swing is a free 4–8 mana body. Protect
her, give her haste, and let the 26 A/D/D bodies she smuggles in end the game.

**Reservation honored:** she was pinned out of The Ur-Dragon for exactly this deck
(pins.csv, 2026-08-15). The pin resolves here.

## Engine notes (do not cut)
- The A/D/D count is the deck: **26 bodies** (13 angels, 9 demons, the dragons Smaug's
  deck didn't claim). Cutting a fatty for a value spell breaks her math — she needs one
  in hand EVERY turn.
- **Dragons went to Smaug, Wicked Worm first** (built the same day; treasure/artifact
  dragons belong there). If a game wants more dragons here, that's the buylist —
  duplicates, per the player.
- Draw runs hot (19) and ramp light (8) in the draft build on purpose: her free drops
  ARE the ramp once she connects. Re-check after the first optimizer pass with real
  field data.

## Mulligan for
A protected turn-4 Kaalia: land, land, land, rock/haste piece, one big body to drop.
No A/D/D in hand = ship it.
