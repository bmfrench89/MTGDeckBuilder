# The Plan
Mono-black **discard**, built 2026-10-15 at the player's request ("build Tinybones"). The
idea came from the player's Argon conversation ("Zero-Byte Hand Sadism"). Every piece it
named was checked against the collection: all 19 are owned and free.

**Commander (verified):** Tinybones, Bauble Burglar — {1}{B}, Legendary Creature — Skeleton
Rogue, 1/3.
*"Whenever an opponent discards a card, exile it from their graveyard with a stash counter
on it. During your turn, you may play cards you don't own with stash counters on them from
exile, and mana of any type can be spent to cast those spells. {3}{B}, {T}: Each opponent
discards a card. Activate only as a sorcery."*

**The Stash.** Every card an opponent discards becomes a card you can play, and the
punishers charge them for it on the way out. The deck does three things:
- **Make them discard every turn:** Tinybones himself, the two-mana discard creatures, The
  Raven Man, Liliana of the Veil, Necrogoyf, and the discard spells.
- **Charge for every discard:** Megrim, Liliana's Caress, Raiders' Wake, Fell Specter and
  Entropic Battlecruiser make them lose life. Waste Not and Geth's Grimoire pay you.
- **Spend the stash.** You cast their cards with any mana.

Tinybones, Pocket Nuisance plays in the 99, as the commander option this one beat.

# How it wins
- **The punishers.** Each opponent discard, with these out:
  - Megrim: 2 damage.
  - Liliana's Caress, Raiders' Wake and Fell Specter: 2 life each.
  - Entropic Battlecruiser: 3 life, at station 1+. Tap any 1-power creature once.
  - Waste Not: a 2/2 Zombie for a creature card, {B}{B} for a land, a card for anything
    else.
  - Geth's Grimoire: a card.

  A single Burglar Rat with Megrim and the Caress out is 12 life across a three-opponent
  table.
- **Dark Deal is the burst.** Each player discards their hand, then draws that many minus
  one. With Megrim and the Caress out, an opponent holding five cards loses 20. Against
  three opponents holding five each, all fifteen discards go to your stash and Waste Not
  pays for every one. You redraw your own hand less one.
- **Hellbent clock.** Once their hands are empty, the deck keeps paying:
  - Bandit's Talent level 2 makes each opponent with one or fewer cards lose 2 at their
    upkeep. Level 3 draws you an extra card for each of them.
  - Asylum Visitor draws you a card whenever a player starts their upkeep with no cards.
  - Aclazotz and Refurbished Familiar draw you a card for each opponent who can't
    discard.
- **Gray Merchant of Asphodel** drains by devotion: Waste Not, Megrim, Bandit's Talent,
  Oppression, Painful Quandary and the creatures are all black pips.
- **Pocket Nuisance** deals 1 damage to each opponent whenever ANY player discards one or
  more cards, your own discards included.

# The creative lines
- **Order the triggers (Tergrid).** When an opponent discards a permanent card, Tergrid
  and Tinybones both trigger, and you choose the order.
  - Put Tergrid's on top: the card comes straight onto the battlefield under your control,
    and Tinybones' exile then finds nothing.
  - Put Tinybones' on top: it goes to the stash, to be played later.

  Choose per card: a permanent you want now goes to Tergrid, and everything else goes to
  the stash.
- **Gonti makes stealing pay for itself.** "Whenever a player casts a spell they don't
  own, that player creates a Treasure token." Every stashed spell you cast makes you a
  Treasure. A stashed land is played, not cast, so it makes none.
- **Discard your own wipe.** Sandman's Quicksand has mayhem: "You may cast this card from
  your graveyard for {3}{B} if you discarded it this turn." Discard it to your own
  Oppression, Dark Deal, Delirium Skeins or Liliana's +1, then cast it the same turn. Cast
  for mayhem, it gives only your opponents' creatures −2/−2.
- **Mindslicer** empties every hand when it dies. Sacrifice it with Village Rites (an
  instant), Plaguecrafter or Rankle while the punishers are out: every opponent's whole
  hand becomes damage and stash.
- **Pit Automaton** taps for {C}{C} that can pay only for activated abilities. This deck
  activates every turn: Tinybones' and The Raven Man's {3}{B}, Bandit's Talent's levels,
  Geier Reach Sanitarium.
- **Geier Reach Sanitarium** ({2}, {T}: each player draws, then discards) is a discard
  source on a land. Every opponent's discard triggers the whole engine.

# Mulligan for
- 2-4 lands plus a two-mana discard creature or an early punisher (Waste Not, Megrim,
  Liliana's Caress, Bandit's Talent).
- Tinybones on turn 2 is the plan. His {3}{B} activation every turn from turn 4 is the
  baseline discard.

# Sequencing tips
- **Punishers before discard.** A Burglar Rat on turn 2 gets one card. The same Rat after
  Megrim and the Caress is 12 life.
- **The symmetric cards hit you too:**
  - Oppression: whoever casts a spell discards a card.
  - Dark Deal, Delirium Skeins, Mind Rake's overload, Liliana's +1, Necrogoyf: every
    player discards.

  Cast them when your hand is empty or worse than theirs, and keep Sandman's Quicksand in
  mind as the card to discard.
- **Play the stash before it rots.** You may play stashed cards only during your turn.
  Spend it every turn, and remember Gonti's Treasure on each cast.
- **Tinybones Joins Up** makes target players each mill a card and lose 1 life whenever a
  legendary creature you control enters. Those creatures are Tinybones (each recast),
  Pocket Nuisance, Tergrid, Gonti, The Raven Man, Rankle, Tourach and Aclazotz.
- **Witch's Cottage** enters untapped with three other Swamps and puts a creature card from
  your graveyard on top of your library. Memorial to Folly returns Gray Merchant or
  Mindslicer to hand.

# Weaknesses to respect
- **The engine is enchantments.** Waste Not, Megrim, the Caress, Bandit's Talent,
  Oppression and Painful Quandary all die to one enchantment wipe. Don't run more of them
  out than the table needs to see.
- **Madness and flashback players like discarding.** Bojuka Bog exiles a graveyard, and
  Tinybones' stash takes their discards out of it anyway.
- **Tinybones is a 1/3** with a sorcery-speed ability. He dies to anything, but he costs
  two to recast.
- **Wheels refill them.** A table that draws seven undoes a turn of discard. Hold Dark
  Deal for after it.
- **Protection is thin:** Imp's Mischief can redirect a removal spell aimed at the
  commander, and that's all. The power check reads 1 protection and 0 recursion.
